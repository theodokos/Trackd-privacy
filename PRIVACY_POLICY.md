# Privacy Policy for Trackd

**Effective Date:** December 24, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how **Trackd** (the "Application") handles your data.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
Trackd is designed with privacy as a core principle. The Application collects the following types of data:
*   **Billable Hour Logs:** Dates, hours worked, and optional notes.
*   **Work Schedule:** Your target hours, workdays, and planned vacations.
*   **Holidays:** Custom holidays you add to the Application.

This data is used solely to provide the Application's core functionality: calculating your billable progress and generating recovery plans.

## 2. Data Storage (CloudKit)
Trackd uses Apple's **CloudKit** service to store and sync your data across your personal devices. 
*   Your data is stored in your private iCloud database.
*   **We (the developer) do not have access to your data.**
*   Apple's privacy policy and security measures govern the storage of this data. You can manage your iCloud data through your device settings.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The Application does not share your data with any third-party services, analytics providers, or advertising networks.

## 4. Your Rights
Since all data is stored locally on your device or in your personal iCloud account, you have full control over it. You can:
*   **Export your data:** Use the built-in export feature to save a JSON backup of your records.
*   **Delete your data:** Deleting the Application or removing data within the app will remove it from your device and iCloud.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
